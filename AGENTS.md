# Dexite design direction

Use the approved Marathon Art Style Study for Dexite as the design handbook for all future Dexite website work.
Reference: https://chatgpt.com/library?file_id=libfile_26d9aaf0bf40819187426b4b698bd45b&account_id=personal
Local reference: ../../Brand/Research/Marathon-Art-Style-Study-for-Dexite.html

- Expressive typography, deliberate asymmetry, ink #090A09, lime #DEFF00, warm paper #F1F0E9.
- Keep Manrope for readable interface/body text and Oswald for display; do not add fonts without a purpose.
- One dominant statement per section. Every label and decoration must earn its place. No fake telemetry or arbitrary identifiers.
- Use real SVG icons, accessible names, semantic controls, visible focus, and 44px interactive targets.
- Hover and press feedback should take 120–180ms; move only small icons or paint, not the surrounding layout. Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
- Make inquiry and booking behavior honest: opening an email draft is not submission, and an inquiry is not a confirmed booking.
- Prioritize readable mobile layouts, persistent field labels, and keyboard usability over decorative density.
- Use original Dexite branding, never copied Marathon logos, faction marks, or game art as production UI assets.
